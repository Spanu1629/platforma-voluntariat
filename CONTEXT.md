# CONTEXT — platforma-voluntariat vocabulary

<!-- graffino-agentic:begin v1 -->
> The approved terms for this project. Use each term exactly as defined, in the part
> of speech given. Never swap in a synonym; never use a word on an _Avoid_ line.
<!-- graffino-agentic:end -->

**Volunteer** (noun) — A person participating in the organization’s volunteering activities.
_Avoid_: helper, user when referring specifically to a volunteer.

**Coordinator** (noun) — A person managing volunteering activities through the coordinator interface.
_Avoid_: administrator when referring specifically to a coordinator.

**Event** (noun) — A volunteering activity managed by coordinators.
_Avoid_: campaign when referring to an event.

**Shift** (noun) — A scheduled volunteering period associated with an event.
_Avoid_: event when referring to a shift.

**Registration** (noun) — A volunteer’s registration for a volunteering opportunity; exact rules remain unconfirmed.
_Avoid_: attendance when referring to registration.

**Waiting list** (noun) — A list of volunteers awaiting a place; exact ordering rules remain unconfirmed.
_Avoid_: registration when referring to waiting-list status.

**Attendance** (noun) — A record of volunteering participation.
_Avoid_: registration when referring to attendance.

**Check-in** (noun) — A recorded arrival using the planned QR attendance workflow.
_Avoid_: registration when referring to arrival.

**Check-out** (noun) — A recorded departure using the planned QR attendance workflow.
_Avoid_: check-in when referring to departure.

**Volunteering hours** (noun) — Time tracked for volunteering activities; calculation rules remain unconfirmed.
_Avoid_: points when referring to time.

**Training record** (noun) — Information about a volunteer’s training; required fields remain unconfirmed.
_Avoid_: certificate when referring to training records.

**Volunteering certificate** (noun) — A PDF document certifying volunteering; its content and issuance rules remain unconfirmed.
_Avoid_: training record when referring to a volunteering certificate.

**Partner organization** (noun) — The real volunteer organization supplying project requirements and potentially initial data.
_Avoid_: university when referring to the volunteer organization.

These terms describe the supplied scope. They do not settle open business rules.
